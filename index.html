Questions

Do I need a new Instagram account?+

No. Aurora connects to your existing Instagram professional (Business or Creator) account through Meta's official API. Same handle, same followers, same message history.

What happens when it doesn't know the answer?+

It tells the customer someone from the team will follow up, then flags the chat for you in the inbox and by notification.

Will it send spammy messages?+

No. It only replies to people who comment or message you first, and reminders go only to customers who booked or were invoiced.

Which tools does it work with?+

Instagram, Messenger, Google Calendar, Calendly, QuickBooks, Stripe, SMS and email. Need something else? Tell us on the call and we'll build it.

How long does setup take?+

Most businesses go live within a week. We set up your keywords, answers, booking rules and invoice reminders, then test everything with you.

How much does it cost?+

Every business needs something different, so we quote after a free call. Book a time and we'll map out which automations make sense for you.
